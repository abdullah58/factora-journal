Privacy Policy

Your privacy, in plain language.

Last updated: September 20, 2026

factora Journal (“we,” “our,” “us”), operated by Emilio Lopez in Coral Gables, Florida, respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and what rights you have. The site is a simple editorial publication; we collect the minimum necessary to run it.

1. Information we collect

  • Email address. If you subscribe to our newsletter (“The Evidence File”), we store the email address you provide, solely to send the newsletter.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically logs standard technical information such as IP addresses, browser type, and pages visited, for security and performance. We do not use this to identify you personally.
  • Correspondence. If you email us, we keep your message so we can reply.

We do not collect names, phone numbers, payment details, or precise location data. We do not run user accounts.

2. Cookies

We use only strictly necessary, privacy-respecting storage required for the site to function. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. See our Cookie Policy for details.

3. How we use your information

  • To send the newsletter you subscribed to, and to manage unsubscribes.
  • To reply to messages you send us.
  • To keep the site secure and performing well.

We never sell your personal information. We never share it with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Third parties

  • Hosting: the site is hosted by Vercel, which processes technical logs on our behalf.
  • Newsletter signup: the subscribe form on this site is currently a front-end demo — it does not transmit or store your email address anywhere. When we connect a real email delivery provider, this policy will be updated first.

5. Data retention

Newsletter email addresses are kept until you unsubscribe, at which point they are removed from the mailing list. Correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to it. To exercise any of these rights, email factorajournal@proton.me. We will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Children

factora Journal is written to be readable by curious readers of all ages, but our newsletter is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their email addresses. If you believe a child has subscribed, contact us and we will remove the address.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. The “Last updated” date above will always reflect the current version. Material changes will be noted in the newsletter.

9. Contact

For any privacy question or request: factorajournal@proton.me.
