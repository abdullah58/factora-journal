The Only Animal That Poops Cubes

Wombats are the only known animals that produce cube-shaped droppings — and the reason involves intestinal physics that won an Ig Nobel.

By Emilio Lopez · September 19, 2026

Among all the world’s animals, only the wombat produces cube-shaped poop. It isn’t sculpted after the fact; the cubes form inside the intestine, where sections of gut stretch at different rates and mold the waste into six-sided blocks. The finding comes from peer-reviewed findings by a Georgia Tech team that studied the intestinal tissue under strain — work that earned the 2019 Ig Nobel Prize in Physics.

Why cubes? Wombats stack their droppings to mark territory, and cubes don’t roll away. Round pellets would tumble off the rocks and logs where wombats leave their messages. It’s a small masterpiece of evolutionary engineering, documented in zoological records alongside the wombat’s other oddity: a backward-facing pouch that keeps dirt off the joey while mom digs.

How we know: Hu et al. (2018) on intestinal biomechanics, plus field observations of wombat latrine-stacking behavior in Tasmania.
