About Us

About factora Journal and Emilio Lopez, its founder and editor.

factora Journal is an independent journal about how we know what we know. Every entry starts from a remarkable fact and reconstructs the trail behind it — the experiment, the accident, the argument, the archive — that turned a question into knowledge.

The journal was founded by Emilio Lopez on a simple conviction: a fact without its evidence is just a rumor with good branding. factora Journal exists to show the work — to name the sources, describe the tests, and admit what is still uncertain.

What we do

  • Publish short, carefully checked investigations into remarkable facts from history, science, nature, and culture.
  • Show the evidence trail for every claim, so readers can verify rather than merely trust.
  • Write in plain language — no jargon without translation, no hype without substance.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t publish breaking news or hot takes. The journal is built to be worth reading years from now.
  • We don’t sell reader data, run intrusive advertising, or optimize for outrage.
  • We don’t present speculation as fact. When the evidence is thin, we say so.

About the author

Emilio Lopez is the founder and editor of factora Journal. Based in Coral Gables, Florida, he brings long experience writing and editing fact journals — tracking down the evidence behind the stories most publications merely repeat. He writes, researches, and fact-checks every entry himself, and he answers reader mail personally. At home, his toughest fact-checkers are his two sons, Max and Alex. If you spot an error — or a fact worth investigating — he wants to hear about it.
