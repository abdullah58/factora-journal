Cookie Policy

Almost no tracking here.

Last updated: September 20, 2026

factora Journal keeps tracking to a minimum. This policy explains the limited storage technologies the Site uses.

1. What we use

  • Strictly necessary storage. Technical data required for the site to load and function (e.g. security and load-balancing by our host, Vercel). These cannot be disabled without breaking the site, and they are not used to track you across the web.
  • Your preferences. If you interact with site features (such as dismissing a notice), a small local preference may be stored in your browser.

2. What we don’t use

  • No advertising cookies.
  • No cross-site trackers.
  • No social-media pixels.

If we ever add analytics or other optional cookies, this policy will be updated first, and — where required by law — we will ask for your consent before setting them.

3. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary storage may affect how the Site functions. Instructions vary by browser; search your browser’s help for “manage cookies.”

4. Contact

Cookie questions: factorajournal@proton.me.
