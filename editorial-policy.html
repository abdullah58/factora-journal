Editorial Policy

The editorial standards behind every factora Journal entry.

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Trust is the whole product. This policy describes the standards every factora Journal entry is held to.

1. Verification

  • Every factual claim in an entry is checked against at least one reliable primary or high-quality secondary source before publication.
  • Where evidence is contested or thin, the entry says so explicitly instead of smoothing it over.
  • Each entry includes a “How we know” note naming what it leans on, so readers can check our work.

2. Corrections

  • If an error is found after publication, we correct the entry promptly.
  • Material corrections are noted visibly on the entry with the date of the correction.
  • Readers can report errors to [CONTACT EMAIL]. Every report is read by the editor.

3. Independence

  • Entries are written independently. No entry is sponsored, and no advertiser influences what we publish.
  • If that ever changes, any commercial relationship will be disclosed clearly on the affected entry.

4. What we won’t publish

  • Unverified viral claims, presented as fact.
  • Content designed to mislead, even “for engagement.”
  • Plagiarized or unattributed work. Sources are credited; words are our own.

5. Authorship

All entries are written and edited by Emilio Lopez, founder and editor of factora Journal.
